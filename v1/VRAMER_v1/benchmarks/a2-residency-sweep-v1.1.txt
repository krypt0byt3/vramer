MRU retains a stable resident prefix across passes, so it reaches within ~1 point
of the theoretical optimum for a cyclic scan. For token-by-token model-layer
streaming this is the difference between re-streaming the whole model every token
(LRU) and keeping the resident fraction hot (MRU). Lock-free source reads give the
single demand thread a 1-deep load/compute pipeline, so baseline streaming also
improved (~3.09s -> ~2.90s on the 16GiB/4GiB single-pass case).
